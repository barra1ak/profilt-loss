05 • Protect it

What would you trade?

Margin is not sacred. But giving it up should buy something valuable.

Protect margin deliberately—not blindly.

Choose how you'd respond. There isn't always one perfect answer. The point is to understand what you're giving up, and what you're getting in return.

06 • Remember it

The principle

Margin funds everything that comes after the sale.

Why protect margin?

Protect margin deliberately. Give it up only when you understand what you're getting in return.

Margin helps pay for people, trucks, facilities, service, technology, safety, reinvestment and future growth. When margin disappears accidentally, the business has fewer choices. Strategic tradeoffs can make sense—but they should be conscious tradeoffs.
